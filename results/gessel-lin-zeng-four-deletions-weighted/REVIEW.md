# Independent AI review of the written four-deletion certificate

## Exact claim

For Jacobi–Stirling words on the ordered barred and unbarred labels through 31, use two copies of each unbarred label, delete four distinct barred labels, and require every letter between the copies of an unbarred label to exceed that label. Write E and F for the internal-descent polynomials for the sets {1,2,3,4} and {28,29,30,31}. Write A for the sum over all four-deletion sets. The proof gives a nonreal zero for E+900F and for A+10⁶(E+900F). The second witness assigns positive integer weights of 1,000,001 and 900,000,001 to the two extreme sets and 1 to each remaining set. Multiplying by the fixed final-descent factor x preserves the nonreal zeros. The equal-weight conjecture has a separate quantifier. [Gessel–Lin–Zeng, Theorem 2 and Conjecture 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1201.0622v2) · [Ma–Wang, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03111v1#S4)

## Weakest step

The sign certificate depends on the insertion length and on applying the same count to the precise admissible words. The two copies of the maximum unbarred label must be adjacent. Remove the pair and then its barred copy when present. In the inverse operation the barred copy and the pair are successive maximum insertions. Inserting either into a descent gap replaces that descent by an ascent followed by a descent, preserving the count. The terminal gap preserves it, and any other gap increases it by one. The pair contributes no internal descent. The empty word has one preserving gap. This gives the stated operator, including the length shift when a barred copy is present. Rank two gives the reduced polynomials 1+7x+4x² and 1+5x+2x² for deleting the first and second barred labels, respectively. The source counts a terminal descent on each nonempty word, which explains its extra factor of x.

## Independent check

I reviewed the exact submitted proof and checker as the second research session. I replayed the submitted checker in a fresh directory with isolated Python. Independently, I worked in Taylor coordinates at x=−23 throughout the insertion, without forming the monomial coefficient vectors or taking numerical derivatives. In the variable y=x+23, the derivative multiplier is −552+47y−y². Thus the Taylor coefficient of degree j updates by adding the three contributions from degrees j−1, j and j+1. For A I propagated five states for zero through four deletions and added the two transitions at each label, grouping the subsets by their last decision. This reproduced both exact negative Laguerre differences from the submitted checker. The equal-weight control has a positive difference at −23. Scaling the strictly positive witness by 3 scales its difference by 9. The constant coefficient equals 901031465, which also follows by summing the 31,465 weights. Replacing the evaluation point by 0 in a copy of the submitted checker makes it fail the negative-difference assertion. The product formula for real linear factors proves that the negative sign excludes real splitting, including when there would be repeated roots.

## Verdict

Passed for the two rank-31 weighted counterexamples. The strictly positive construction is proved by the exact written certificate. The separate pinned Lean development covers the two-term witness through the insertion recurrence, including the source factor of x. This proof gives an obstruction to extending the arbitrary-weight theorem to four deletions. The all-rank equal-weight question remains open.
